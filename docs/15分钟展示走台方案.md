# 香港就业市场探索器 · 15 分钟展示审核稿

## 时间判断

旧稿逐张讲 20 张图，还安排了多次点选、改值和回滚。它可以做素材库。直接按它切图，再加英文报数和交接，15 分钟会很紧。建议用下列路线实讲 **13 分 20 秒**，留 **1 分 40 秒** 处理换人、页面跳转和意外停顿。这个时间是排练目标，实际用时取决于口语和操作速度。

主线是：**嘉宁带着一个问题进来，每看一张主图就更新一个判断，下一章追问这个判断。** 最后演示同一人改一个条件，看到不同结果。观众需要看懂探索器如何帮助她决定下一步。

## 主讲路线

| 时段 | 嘉宁在探索什么 | 主讲画面与操作 | 讲完后嘉宁怎样做 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | 我毕业后在哪里工作？ | 英文首页。用一句话交代她在香港毕业、也考虑内地。 | 先找值得继续查的行业。 |
| 1:00–2:25 | 各行业 35 岁及以上就业占比有什么差异？ | 00 页成对条形图，保持较新截面。只指资讯通讯和住宿餐饮两行，说明两地相差一年。 | 把行业结构当作线索，转去查香港本地岗位。 |
| 2:25–3:55 | 行业名称能变成什么职位搜索词？ | 01 页堆叠面积图和职业树图，只用 2025 年。两图之间讲一句「行业是雇主做什么，职业是岗位做什么」。 | 用行业与职业的组合搜职位。 |
| 3:55–5:25 | 刚毕业的年龄段要预留多久？ | 02 页年龄热力图，指 2025 年的 20–24 岁和 25–29 岁。 | 给求职计划留缓冲，并行投递。 |

## 建议小组审核的三项取舍

1. **让 04 页成为唯一完整的现场操作。** 00–03 各讲一个发现和一个后续决定，所有图保留供追问。危机对齐、教育、参与率、树图联动和预算瀑布的详细读法可以留在问答。
2. **把“42 改成 44”固定为高潮。** 42 小时下有两个方向满足三条条件，44 小时下地产活动成为第三项。观众能直接看到探索器如何检验一个假设。时间宽裕时，可加演把专业及科研技术的 HK$29,500 月薪中位数代入预算，得到 HK$11,000 估算结余。
3. **确定交接与超时预案。** 若多人讲，建议开场及 00–01、02–03、04 及结论各由一人连讲，页面由同一人控制。到 07:30 还没进入 04，直接打开该页，先载入嘉宁方案，随后只演示 42 → 44。演示前用英文计时跑两次，第二次以 13:20 内讲完为目标。

## English cue lines

### Opening

“Jia Ning has just finished her master’s degree in Hong Kong. She could apply here or on the Mainland. Follow the choices she makes in the Explorer. At each step, a chart helps her decide what to ask next.”

### Two markets → Employment landscape

“The age profiles tell her where to look next. In the more recent snapshot, 61.4 percent of Hong Kong workers in information and communications were aged 35 and over, compared with 70.1 percent in accommodation and food. The two markets are measured a year apart, and the Mainland breakdown uses project estimates. Jia Ning takes the pattern as a lead and turns to the Hong Kong employment landscape.”

“Here she separates industries from occupations. That lets her search for the same type of work across different employers.”

### Unemployment and recovery → Gender and earnings

“Next, she asks how much time to allow. In 2025, unemployment was 11.2 percent for ages 20 to 24 and 5.2 percent for ages 25 to 29. She builds in some time and applies to several employers in parallel.”

“The earnings figures also need context. Industry medians cover different roles, hours and levels of experience. Jia Ning writes down two interview questions: What pay band applies to this role? What are the typical working hours?”

### Explore job options

“Let’s select retail on the hiring chart. The vacancy mix, pay, hours, history and shortlist follow that selection. For retail, the median is 16,000 Hong Kong dollars per month and 48 working hours per week.”

“Now load Jia Ning’s example. She is testing four broad industries against three criteria: at least 25,000 a month, no more than 42 hours a week, and at least 6,000 left after expenses. Financing and insurance and professional, scientific and technical services meet all three.”

“Real estate activities miss the hours limit. What if she can accept 44 hours? Change the number, then select ‘Apply criteria’. Real estate now appears as a third option. The chart data are the same. Her shortlist changed because her assumption changed.”

### Close

“Jia Ning can now name two directions to investigate first, one option to reconsider if her hours limit is flexible, and what to ask when she receives a real offer. That is the decision the Explorer helps her reach today. When she has new information, she can run the exploration again.”
